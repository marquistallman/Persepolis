Frequently Asked Questions

Is PersepolisAI free to use?

Yes! You can browse and download thousands of standard resolution wallpapers for free. We also offer a Premium tier for 8K downloads and exclusive collections.

How do I download a wallpaper?

Simply click on any wallpaper you like to open the preview, then click the "Download" button. The image will open in a new tab or start downloading automatically.

Can I use these images for commercial projects?

It depends on the license of the specific image. Most wallpapers are for personal use only. Please check the licensing details on the image page or our Licensing section.

How does the AI search work?

Our AI analyzes the content, color palette, and mood of images to provide more accurate search results. You can also use our Chat feature to describe exactly what you're looking for in natural language.

I'm a creator, how can I upload?

We are currently accepting creator applications. Please contact us via the Contact page with your portfolio to be considered for our Verified Creator program.
